How a loopy gets made and run

Six layouts, one story. You patch a lodash CVE once in Codex (loopy import), Loopy pulls six nodes out of the fifteen trace events (loopy extract), then the graph runs on your machine (loopy run). The checks fail once and retry. The push waits for your approval.

Same trace, same nodes, same 18 second clock. What changes is where the trace lives and how the nodes arrange themselves. Each card has a switch for how nodes enter: outline draws the border first, morph grows the trace line into the card, drift fades the card in with a small lift.
